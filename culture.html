Tea Heritage

Longjing Tea Village Tasting Route

Hands-on tea tasting and village walk in the Dragon Well production area around West Lake.

  • Suggested time: 2-3 hours
  • Best season: Spring tea period
  • Booking tip: Reserve guided tasting in advance

National Museum

China National Tea Museum

Bilingual exhibitions on Chinese tea history, production, and cultural practices in a scenic tea setting.

  • Suggested time: 1.5-2.5 hours
  • Ideal for: Families and first-time tea learners
  • Tip: Pair with nearby tea hillside route

Textile Culture

Silk Culture Storytelling & Craft Walk

Learn how Hangzhou's silk legacy shaped trade and aesthetics, then join light craft or showroom experiences.

  • Suggested time: 1.5-2 hours
  • Logistics: Best arranged as guided stop
  • Tip: Confirm demonstration times before visiting

Buddhist Culture

Lingyin Temple Ritual Culture

Observe incense rituals, Buddhist architecture, and mountain spirituality in one of Hangzhou's defining heritage sites.

  • Suggested time: 2 hours
  • Etiquette: Quiet dress and respectful behavior
  • Tip: Morning visits are usually calmer

Poetic Heritage

Ten Scenes of West Lake Culture Route

Follow the classical viewpoint concept that influenced East Asian garden and landscape aesthetics.

  • Suggested time: Half day
  • Transport: Segment route by taxi + walking
  • Tip: Sunset slot for Leifeng and lakeside scenes

Performance

West Lake Night Performance Option

A visual-night experience inspired by Hangzhou landscape culture, often used as a premium evening add-on.

  • Suggested time: 1-1.5 hours
  • Booking: Reserve seats 1-3 days ahead in peak periods
  • Tip: Check weather and cancellation rules

Canal Life

Grand Canal Urban Culture Walk

Mix of local daily life, heritage blocks, and modern creative spaces along the Hangzhou canal section.

  • Suggested time: 2-3 hours
  • Ideal for: Slow travelers and photographers
  • Tip: Start late afternoon for day-to-night change

Culinary Culture

Jiangnan Food Heritage Tasting

Taste-oriented cultural route around classic Hangzhou flavors with storytelling on seasonal cuisine.

  • Suggested time: 2 hours
  • Best slot: Dinner window
  • Tip: Add dietary restrictions in booking notes

  • Format: Small group/private studio
  • Tip: Confirm language support before booking
