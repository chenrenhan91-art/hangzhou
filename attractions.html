Top Hangzhou Attractions

Click any card to open its English introduction page in a new tab (Wikipedia or English reference with text and images).

UNESCO Core

West Lake Cultural Landscape

The signature Hangzhou icon with causeways, islands, and poetic viewpoints. Ideal sunrise or sunset loop by foot and short boat rides.

  • Suggested time: 3-4 hours
  • Transport: Metro + lakeside walk/short taxi hops
  • Ticket tip: Lake area open; boat tickets vary by route

Buddhist Heritage

Lingyin Temple & Feilai Peak

One of the region's best-known Buddhist temple complexes with grotto carvings and mountain ambience.

  • Suggested time: 2-3 hours
  • Transport: Taxi from West Lake south-west edge
  • Ticket tip: Scenic area and temple are often separate fees

Skyline View

Leifeng Pagoda

A rebuilt landmark tied to the Legend of the White Snake. Great panoramic lake view from upper levels.

  • Suggested time: 1-1.5 hours
  • Transport: Easy stop on South Lake route
  • Ticket tip: Scenic ticket required, hours vary seasonally

Tea Landscape

Longjing Village & Tea Terraces

Classic Dragon Well tea fields and village trails. Best in spring picking season and mild autumn weather.

  • Suggested time: 2-3 hours
  • Transport: Taxi or private car from lake area
  • Ticket tip: Village access is usually free; tasting prices vary

City Viewpoint

Six Harmonies Pagoda (Liuhe Pagoda)

Historic pagoda overlooking Qiantang River, popular for architecture and broad river-city perspective.

  • Suggested time: 1-1.5 hours
  • Transport: South city route, combine with pagoda belt
  • Ticket tip: Entry fee applies; top view access may have rules

Museum Stop

Zhejiang Provincial Museum (West Lake Area)

Solid option for regional history and artifacts, especially useful in rainy weather schedules.

  • Suggested time: 1.5-2 hours
  • Transport: Near core West Lake circuit
  • Ticket tip: Check timed entry policy and ID requirements

Nature Walk

Botanical Garden & Yanggong Causeway Belt

A quieter green route for slow-paced travelers, ideal for spring flowers and low-crowd photo walks.

  • Suggested time: 2 hours
  • Transport: Short taxi transfer from north-west lake side
  • Ticket tip: Garden ticket may apply

Night Walk

Hefang Street & Southern Song Imperial Street

Historic-commercial streets for local snacks, heritage facades, and easy evening atmosphere.

  • Suggested time: 1.5-2 hours
  • Transport: Metro friendly, good post-dinner route
  • Ticket tip: No entry fee, spending depends on food/shopping

Canal Heritage

Grand Canal (Hangzhou Section)

Urban heritage corridor with museums and waterfront walks, suitable for half-day city culture extension.

  • Suggested time: 2-3 hours
  • Transport: Metro + short walk transfer
  • Ticket tip: Most public areas are free

Art Option

China Academy of Art Area (Xiangshan + Nanshan)

For architecture lovers and design-focused visitors; pair with tea stops for a modern cultural route.

  • Suggested time: 1.5-2.5 hours
  • Transport: Best by taxi or pre-arranged driver
  • Ticket tip: Some spaces are public, exhibitions may be ticketed
